## Goal
Using the research notes at ceo/researcher/work/research_notes.md, produce a complete, decision-ready competitor analysis markdown report at ceo/writer/work/report.md. The report is for a technical audience evaluating self-hosted note-taking / knowledge-base apps. Write in clear, skimmable prose with tables. Do not invent facts — use only what is in the research notes. Every claim must cite the source URL from the research notes. Claims that are flagged UNVERIFIED in the research notes must also be flagged UNVERIFIED in the report. You are a writer, not a researcher: do not add claims beyond what the research provides.

## Acceptance criteria
1. One markdown report at ceo/writer/work/report.md with ALL of these sections (use these exact headings):
   - Executive Summary
   - Methodology and Limitations
   - Landscape Table (all 8 apps: stars, latest release, license, E2EE, collab, deployment)
   - Feature Matrix (editor, organization, search, backlinks, E2EE, collaboration, mobile, plugins/API, import/export — one row per app)
   - Ops/Licensing Comparison (deployment dependencies, resource requirements, backup, auth/SSO, license implications, paid-tier gating, release cadence)
   - Per-App Profiles (one subsection per app with landscape health, key features, self-hosting notes, standout strengths, notable limitations)
   - Persona Recommendations (solo power user, privacy-focused user, small team, org wiki, minimal footprint — with named app recommendations and rationale)
   - Risks (per-app and general risks: governance, license traps, vendor lock-in, maturity)
   - Sources List (all URLs cited in the document)
2. Every factual claim has a source URL (inline citation or footnote); unverified claims are explicitly flagged UNVERIFIED; no invented facts.
3. Report is accurate, concrete, and skimmable. No filler.
