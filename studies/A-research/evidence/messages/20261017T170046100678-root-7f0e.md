## Goal
Produce a decision-ready competitor analysis of self-hosted note-taking / knowledge-base apps (today is 2026-10-17; all facts must be current).
Scope: shortlist 6-8 relevant apps (candidates: Joplin, Trilium/TriliumNext, Standard Notes, Memos, Outline, SiYuan, AFFiNE, AppFlowy, BookStack, Docmost, HedgeDoc, SilverBullet, Notesnook, Logseq; verify relevance and drop/add as evidence warrants).

Suggested team: research specialists using web search/fetch, a writer, and an independent fact-checking reviewer who verifies high-impact claims against primary sources.

3. An independent reviewer has spot-checked at least 6 high-impact claims (licenses, versions, E2EE, collaboration) against primary sources and corrections are applied.
4. Report to root with a short summary and the path of the final report.
