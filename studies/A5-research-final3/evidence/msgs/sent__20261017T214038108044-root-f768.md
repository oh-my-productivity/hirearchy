## Goal
Research Obsidian, Logseq, Joplin, Trilium Notes and AnyType (pricing, license, sync model), write the competitor analysis, have every claim independently fact-checked, apply every required change and report the final file.

Deliverable: A comparison table (one row per app; columns pricing, license, sync model) with a source URL per fact, plus a closing section called "Where we can win" labelled as our assessment (markdown, at most 1500 words). Report its path as an artifact.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Covers exactly the five apps Obsidian, Logseq, Joplin, Trilium Notes and AnyType, and no others.
2. At most 1500 words as counted by read.
3. Contains a comparison table with one row per app and columns for pricing, license and sync model, and a section titled exactly "Where we can win", marked as our assessment (judgement).
4. Every sentence with a number, name, version, license, price, date or "no/only/all" is a factual claim and cites the URL of the page that states it; the fact-checker fetched that page and quoted the sentence.
5. The "Where we can win" section is judgement. It has no citations or new facts and rests only on claims verified elsewhere in the document.
6. The fact-checker's final coverage line reads 'n of m factual claims verified' with n = m (none wrong, unsupported or not-checkable) and 'each resting on verified facts: yes' for its judgement statements, and the writer applied every required change.
7. The final report pastes that coverage line verbatim, with the path of the fact-checker's ledger.
