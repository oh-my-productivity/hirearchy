Fetch the official/primary source page for each fact and record the exact URL. Output a structured markdown file at lead/researcher/work/research.md with all findings and one source URL per fact.

3. Every factual claim (number, name, version, license, price, date, or "no/only/all") cites the URL of the page that states it — the URL must be the live source page you actually fetched.
