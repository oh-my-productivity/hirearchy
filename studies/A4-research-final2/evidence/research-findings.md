We offer optional paid licenses to support our development and provide additional benefits.\" / \"You can download and use Obsidian for free, forever, for any purpose including: Personal use, Commercial use, Non-profit and charitable organizations, Educational institutions, Government departments and agencies\"\n\n### Sync Model\n- **Obsidian Sync**: Proprietary cloud sync service with end-to-end encryption\n- **Alternative**: Self-managed via third-party services (iCloud, OneDrive, Dropbox). Obsidian users can sync with existing storage services.\n\n**Source**: https://obsidian.md/pricing (retrieved 2026-10-17) for Obsidian Sync; https://help.obsidian.md/getting-started/sync-your-notes-across-devices (retrieved 2026-10-17) for third-party sync options  \n**Quote**: \"Sync notes across devices, End-to-end encryption, Version history, Collaborate on shared vaults\" (Obsidian Sync) / \"You can sync your vault using a third-party service like iCloud, OneDrive, Dropbox, or other syncing folders\" (third-party sync support)\n\n---\n\n## JOPLIN\n\n### Pricing Model\n- **Core app**: Free and open-source\n- **Joplin Cloud (optional managed sync)**:\n  - Basic: €2.99/month or €2.40/month (annual) - 2GB storage\n  - Pro: €5.99/month or €4.79/month (annual) - 30GB storage\n  - Pro 100GB: €9.99/month or €7.99/month (annual)\n  - Teams: €7.99/month or €6.69/month (annual, per user, min 2 users)\n- **Joplin Server Business (self-hosted)**: \n  - 2-10 users: €3.33/user/month (€40/year)\n  - 11-50 users: €2.92/user/month (€35/year)\n  - 51+ users: €2.50/user/month (€30/year)\n\n**Source**: https://joplinapp.org/plans (retrieved 2026-10-17)  \n**Quote**: \"Basic 2.99€ /month (95.88€ /year)\" / \"Pro 5.99€ /month\"\n\n### License Type\n- **Desktop/Mobile/CLI apps**: GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPL-3.0)\n- **Joplin Server**: Proprietary Personal Use License (non-commercial only)\n\n**Source**: https://discourse.joplinapp.org/t/joplin-is-switching-to-the-gnu-affero-general-public-license-v3-agpl-3-0/28747 (retrieved 2026-10-17) for desktop/mobile/CLI;
