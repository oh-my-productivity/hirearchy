root changed the contract of service `weekly-meeting-memo` (v2 -> v3). Read it, and follow the new criteria from the next request on:
+ acceptance added: C11: A statement that something stays as it is or is kept unchanged (for example 'Marta keeps the agenda as sent') is not an action item and gets no row in the Action items table.
Acceptance criteria now:
C1: The memo is based only on the requested notes file and contains nothing that is not in it.
C2: At most 400 words as counted by read.
C4: Decisions lists every decision recorded in the notes file.
C5: Action items is a markdown table with the columns Owner | Action | Due, and has one row for every commitment in the notes file, including informal ones stated only in prose (anyone who said they would do something).
C7: Next meeting gives the date of the next meeting as stated in the notes file.
C8: The reviewer's report shows every criterion met (criteria met: n of m with n = m), and the writer applied every required change.
C9: The memo starts with one line that begins with 'Summary:' and sums up the meeting in that single line, followed by exactly these sections, in this order: Decisions, Action items, Next meeting.
C10: Every Due value is an ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD) or none. A deadline in the notes, including a relative one such as 'before Friday' (the date of that Friday, resolved from the meeting date), is turned into its ISO date; a date the commitment is for, such as the date a room is booked for, also counts as its Due. Due is none only when the notes give no such date or say there is no hard date.
C11: A statement that something stays as it is or is kept unchanged (for example 'Marta keeps the agenda as sent') is not an action item and gets no row in the Action items table.
