# Your first week

Welcome to Larkspur Labs! This page explains what the first days look like.

## Before you start
- Send your shipping address to Tomas Lindqvist (laptop).
- Sign your contract in Bamboo.
- Your Google account is created the day before you start.

## Day 1
- Your start time is 14:00 UTC. A 30-minute welcome call with Tomas Lindqvist and your manager starts at that time.
- Set up your laptop, then log into Google, Slack and 1Password.
- Turn on two-factor authentication on every account (see the security policy).
- Join #general, #people-ops and #it-help.
- Introduce yourself in #general with a short message and a photo.

## Days 2 to 5
- Read the handbook in Notion, including the code of conduct.
- Have a 1:1 with your manager every day this week, 30 minutes.
- Your buddy, a colleague from another team, will message you on day 2. Have a virtual coffee together.
- Attend the Friday all-hands at 15:00 UTC, where you will be welcomed.
- Choose your health plan in the Calloway portal (you have 30 days).

## First month
- Complete the security awareness course by the end of week 2.
- Submit home-office stipend receipts within 60 days.
- At 30 days, you have a check-in with your manager about how things are going. Your 90-day goals are set then.

## Who to ask
Anything about the first week: Tomas Lindqvist, timeoff@larkspurlabs.example. Equipment: Dev Anand in #it-help.
