# Time off policy

We trust people to manage their time, and we also want you to actually rest.

## Paid time off (PTO)
Full-time employees receive 25 days of PTO per year, plus public holidays in the country where you live. PTO builds up month by month from your start date, so in your first year you receive a share of the 25 days. You may take PTO as soon as you have earned it.

## How to request
Request time off in the HR tool (Bamboo) at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days. For shorter breaks, tell your manager and put it in the team calendar. Your manager answers within two working days.

## Company shutdown
The whole company closes between 24 December and 1 January. These days do not count against your PTO.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO. You get 10 paid sick days a year. Message your manager before your working day starts if you can. A doctor's note is only needed after 3 days in a row.

## Parental leave
Primary carers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave, and secondary carers receive 6 weeks. Please tell your manager and people-ops at least 8 weeks before the expected start if you can.

## Time zones and availability
Everyone has "core hours" of 13:00 to 17:00 UTC, when we expect you to be reachable. Outside core hours you can arrange your day as you like.
