# Code of conduct

Larkspur Labs is a place where people can do good work and feel respected. This applies in chat, video calls, documents and any in-person meetups.

## Our values
- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Be direct, and give feedback with care.
- Share what you know, and ask when you do not.
- Respect other people's time and time zones.

## What we expect
- Treat everyone with courtesy, whatever their background, role or experience.
- Do not use language or images that demean or exclude people.
- Do not harass anyone. This includes unwelcome comments, repeated unwanted messages and sharing private information.
- Keep video calls professional. Cameras are optional, but please say hello.
- Use inclusive language in chat and documents.

## Communication norms
- Use threads in Slack so conversations stay tidy.
- Reply to direct messages within one working day.
- Write down decisions in Notion so remote colleagues can read them later.
- Meetings always have an agenda. If there is no agenda, you may decline.

## Raising a concern
You can speak to your manager, to People Ops, or use the anonymous form linked in Notion. Reports are reviewed by Marguerite Osei and the CEO, Hana Okafor. Larkspur does not allow retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith. We aim to reply within 3 working days.

## Consequences
Breaking the code of conduct can lead to a warning, or in serious cases, the end of employment.
