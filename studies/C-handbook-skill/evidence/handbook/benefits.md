# Benefits at Larkspur Labs

Larkspur Labs is a team of 40 people working fully remotely. We want everyone to feel looked after, so here is what you get.

## Health cover
Every employee gets health, dental and vision insurance through Calloway Health. Larkspur pays 90% of the monthly premium for you and 70% for dependants. Cover starts on your first day, and you have 30 days from your start date to choose your plan in the Calloway portal.

## Retirement
We offer a retirement savings plan with a 4% company match. You can join after 60 days of employment. The match vests immediately, so it is yours from the first payment.

## Home office stipend
New hires receive a one-time home office stipend of $600 to spend on a desk, chair, monitor or similar. Keep your receipts and submit them through the expense tool within 60 days of your start date. After that, everyone gets $25 per month towards internet costs.

## Learning budget
Each person has a learning budget of $1,200 per calendar year for courses, books and conferences. Your manager must approve anything over $300 before you buy it.

## Wellbeing
We provide a free subscription to the Mindwell meditation app and a confidential counselling line (6 free sessions per year). Details are on the people-ops page.
