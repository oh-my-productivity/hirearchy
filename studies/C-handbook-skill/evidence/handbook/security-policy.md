# Security policy

Keeping customer and company data safe is everyone's job. These rules apply to all staff and contractors.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)
Two-factor authentication is required on every work account (Google, Slack, GitHub, Notion, Bamboo, 1Password). Use an authenticator app or a hardware key. Text-message codes are not allowed. You must turn on 2FA within 48 hours of receiving your account, or the account will be suspended until you do.

## Passwords
Use 1Password to create unique passwords of at least 16 characters. Do not reuse passwords or share them in chat or email.

## Devices
- Keep your laptop's disk encryption on and your screen lock at 5 minutes or less.
- Install system updates within 7 days.
- Use only company laptops for work. Do not work on personal computers.
- Never use public Wi-Fi for work unless you are on the company VPN (Tailscale).

## Confidential information
Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage. Internal documents are marked "Internal" or "Confidential"; do not share these outside Larkspur.

## Phishing
If an email looks odd, do not click anything. Report it with the "Report phishing" button in Google Workspace, or post in #security.

## Reporting an incident
If you lose a laptop or think an account is compromised, tell security@larkspurlabs.example within one hour. You will not be blamed for reporting quickly. Our security lead is Ingrid Bauer.

## Training
Every new hire completes the 45-minute security awareness course within their first 2 weeks. A refresher is required each year.
