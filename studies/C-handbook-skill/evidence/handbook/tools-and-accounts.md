# Tools and accounts

Here is the set of tools we use and how to get access.

## Laptop
Larkspur ships a laptop to every new hire. Tell Tomas Lindqvist your shipping address at least 10 days before your start date. Laptops are sent by courier from our IT partner, and tracking details arrive by email. If your laptop has not arrived 3 working days before your start date, message #it-help. You may choose a Mac or a Linux laptop; Windows is not supported. Please return the laptop within 14 days if you leave the company.

## Accounts you will get
- Email and calendar: Google Workspace. Your address is firstname@larkspurlabs.example. It is created the day before you start.
- Chat: Slack. Join #general, #people-ops and #it-help on day one.
- Video calls: Zoom, with a company licence.
- Documents: Notion is our internal wiki. The handbook lives in Notion.
- Time off and HR: Bamboo.
- Expenses: Expensify.
- Code and projects (engineering and design only): GitHub and Linear.

## Password manager
Everyone uses 1Password. The company pays for it. Never store passwords in a browser or a spreadsheet.

## Getting help
For anything about accounts, laptops or software, post in #it-help. Our IT administrator is Dev Anand (dev@larkspurlabs.example). He replies within one working day, and urgent problems can be marked with the :rotating_light: emoji.
