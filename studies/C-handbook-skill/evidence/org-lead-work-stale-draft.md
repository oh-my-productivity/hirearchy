# Welcome to Larkspur Labs!

We're thrilled to have you joining our fully remote team. This guide will walk you through your first month so you can hit the ground running. Keep this handy and check things off as you go.

## Day 1 Checklist

Welcome! Your day starts at 14:00 UTC with your welcome call [first-week.md].

- **30-minute welcome call** with Tomas Lindqvist and your manager at 14:00 UTC [first-week.md]
- **Set up your laptop** — check that it arrived safely and install any updates [tools-and-accounts.md]
- **Log into your accounts**: Google, Slack and 1Password [first-week.md]
- **Turn on two-factor authentication** on every account (Google, Slack, GitHub, Notion, Bamboo, 1Password). Use an authenticator app or hardware key — text message codes are not allowed [security-policy.md]. You must do this within 48 hours or your account will be suspended [security-policy.md]
- **Join Slack channels**: #general, #people-ops and #it-help [first-week.md]
- **Introduce yourself** in #general with a short message and a photo [first-week.md]

## Week 1 Checklist

You're doing great! Here's what happens this week.

- **Read the handbook** in Notion, including the code of conduct [first-week.md]
- **Learn about communication norms** at Larkspur: use threads in Slack so conversations stay tidy, reply to direct messages within one working day, and write down decisions in Notion so remote colleagues can read them later [code-of-conduct.md]. Meetings always have an agenda — if there is no agenda, you may decline [code-of-conduct.md]
- **Understand our values**: be kind and assume good intent, be direct and give feedback with care, share what you know and ask when you don't, and respect other people's time and time zones [code-of-conduct.md]
- **Have a 1:1 with your manager** every day this week (30 minutes each) [first-week.md]
- **Meet your buddy** — a colleague from another team will message you on day 2. Have a virtual coffee together [first-week.md]
- **Attend Friday all-hands** at 15:00 UTC where you'll be welcomed [first-week.md]
- **Choose your health plan** in the Calloway portal. You have 30 days to select coverage for health, dental and vision [first-week.md] [benefits.md]. Larkspur pays 90% of the premium for you and 70% for dependants [benefits.md]
- **Complete the security awareness course** by the end of week 2 (45 minutes) [first-week.md]
- **Learn about core hours**: 13:00–17:00 UTC is when we expect you to be online. Outside these hours, arrange your day as you like [time-off.md]

## Month 1 Checklist

You're part of the team now. Here's what to wrap up by the end of your first month.

- **Submit home-office stipend receipts** within 60 days. You received a one-time $600 stipend for a desk, chair, monitor or similar. Keep your receipts and submit them through the expense tool [first-week.md] [benefits.md]. After that, you'll receive a monthly internet stipend of $25 per month towards your home office costs [benefits.md]
- **Familiarize yourself with time off**: Full-time employees get 25 days of PTO per year plus public holidays in your country [time-off.md]. PTO builds up month by month, so you get a share in your first year [time-off.md]. Request time off in Bamboo at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days [time-off.md]. For shorter breaks, tell your manager and put it in the team calendar [time-off.md]. You also get 10 paid sick days per year [time-off.md]. A doctor's note is only needed after 3 days in a row [time-off.md]. The whole company closes 24 December–1 January (this doesn't count against your PTO) [time-off.md]
- **Learn about parental leave**: If you are a primary carer, you receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Secondary carers receive 6 weeks [time-off.md]. Tell your manager and people-ops at least 8 weeks before the expected start if you can [time-off.md]
- **Understand the laptop return policy**: If you leave the company, please return your laptop within 14 days [tools-and-accounts.md]
- **30-day check-in with your manager**: At 30 days, you'll meet with your manager to talk about how things are going. This is when your 90-day goals will be set [first-week.md]
- **Retirement savings eligibility**: After your first 60 days of employment, you can join our retirement savings plan with a 4% company match [benefits.md]. The match vests immediately, so it's yours from the first payment [benefits.md]
- **Use your learning budget**: You have $1,200 per calendar year for courses, books and conferences. Your manager must approve anything over $300 before you buy it [benefits.md]
- **Wellbeing resources**: We provide a free subscription to the Mindwell meditation app and 6 free sessions per year with a confidential counselling line [benefits.md]
