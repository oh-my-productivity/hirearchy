## Goal
Deliver the service `weekly-meeting-memo`: One markdown memo of the decisions and action items of one weekly meeting, from the notes file for that meeting.

## Request
- notes_file: 2026-10-19.md

## Acceptance criteria
- C1: Covers only the meeting in the requested notes file, and nothing from other files.
- C2: At most 400 words as counted by read, tables included.
- C3: The memo has a Decisions section, an Action items section and an Open items section.
- C4: Every decision in the notes, including ones agreed only in conversation, is listed once, one line each; no decision that is not in the notes appears.
- C5: Action items are a markdown table with the columns exactly Owner | Action | Due, and every commitment someone made in the notes is a row, including informal ones stated in prose, each with one named owner.
- C6: Every Due value is an ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD) or the word none. A relative or vague deadline is converted to an ISO date, using the meeting date in the notes, when the notes make that date clear; otherwise Due is none.
- C7: Nothing in the memo is missing from or contradicts the notes file; open items are limited to points the notes leave unresolved.
- C8: The reviewer's report shows every criterion met (criteria met: n of m with n = m), and the writer applied every required change.
- C9: The memo starts with a one-line 'Summary:' of the meeting, followed by the Decisions section, then the Action items section.

## Deliverable
One markdown memo named memo-<meeting date>.md, for example memo-2026-10-19.md (format: markdown; at most 400 words; path: /tmp/hirearchy-e7-s3-1792916437/deliverables)
