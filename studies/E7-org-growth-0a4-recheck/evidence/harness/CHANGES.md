## What changed, file by file
- `run_turn.sh` (E7 item 4; `protocol.md` 4.2.1 and 4.5.1):
  - the header label `E6` -> `E7`, and four comment lines that state the boundary and the snapshots;
  - `EV` = `studies/E7-org-growth-0a4-recheck/evidence/s$N`; new `H` = `studies/E7-org-growth-0a4-recheck/evidence/harness` (where
    `ambient_snapshot.sh` is);
  - the profile `hirearchy-study-e6-s$N-assistant` -> `hirearchy-study-e7-s$N-assistant`. The argv is otherwise E6's:
    `kiro-cli chat --v3 --no-interactive --trust-all-tools [--resume] --effort high --agent hirearchy-study-e7-s<N>-assistant "<Lena's line>"`;
  - **the boundary**: E6's three lines (the chat; `rc=$?`; the end times) become one command list,
    `"${CMD[@]}" > ... 2> ...; rc=$?; end=...; endl=...; endu=...; sleep 20`. This is C1's D-HARN form (`kiro-cli chat ... > out 2> err;
    echo $? > exit; sleep 20`, `c1-reproduction.md`, HARa1 and HARa2) with E6's exit-code and end-time capture between the chat and the
    sleep. So the end times are the chat's own, as in E6, and the tool call that runs the script stays open at least 20 s after the chat
    returns. `slept` records when the sleep ended;
  - **the snapshots**: `ambient_snapshot.sh "s$N before turn $T"` into `ambient/turn-NN-before.txt` right before the chat's start time is
    taken, and `ambient_snapshot.sh "s$N after turn $T"` into `ambient/turn-NN-after.txt` after the sleep;
  - the `turn-times.txt` line keeps E6's text up to `exit <rc>` (`extract_mechanical.py` parses that prefix with `re.match`) and appends
    `  sleep 20 ended utc <time>`.
