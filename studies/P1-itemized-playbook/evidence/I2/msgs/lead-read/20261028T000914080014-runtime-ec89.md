Acceptance criteria now:
C1: The memo is based on the requested notes file only.
C2: At most 400 words as counted by read.
C3: The memo has a section titled "Decisions" with one bullet for every decision stated in the notes, and a section titled "Actions" containing a markdown table with exactly the columns Owner | Action | Due; it has no other sections besides a title that names the meeting date.
C4: The Actions table has one row for every commitment in the notes, including informal ones said in prose (for example 'X said he'd do Y'), and no row for anything nobody committed to.
C5: Due is an ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD). A relative deadline such as 'by Friday' is converted to a date using the meeting date when the notes make the date clear. Due is 'none' when the notes give no date or say none.
C6: Every decision and action is traceable to the notes, and nothing is invented.
C7: The reviewer's report shows every criterion met (criteria met: n of m with n = m), and the writer applied every required change.
C8: The memo has a one-line 'Summary:' of the meeting directly after the title, then the Decisions section, then the Actions section; the Summary line is the only addition to what the "Decisions"/"Actions" criterion allows.
C10: Owners are shown as full names, always: in the Actions table the Owner column holds the owner's full name as written in the notes, never initials.
C11: For an owner the notes give only as initials, the Owner cell holds the initials followed by '(full name not in the notes)', for example 'JD (full name not in the notes)'.
Rule (c): a playbook item may be the own words of a criterion added by an amendment the client confirmed (revise_playbook op="add": a step with that text, or a rule with it as then and no when; evidence: its id); confirmed now: C8, C10, C11.
