root changed the contract of service `weekly-meeting-memo` (v4 -> v5). Read it, and follow the new criteria from the next request on:
+ acceptance added: C15: In the Actions table, when the notes give an owner only as initials, the Owner cell reads those initials followed by "(full name not in the notes)", for example "JD (full name not in the notes)". Names given in full stay as written.
The client's yes to R4 (about C14) is recorded: C15 is client-confirmed.
Acceptance criteria now:
C1: Covers only the meeting in the requested notes file.
C2: The memo is markdown and has at most 400 words as counted by read.
C3: Every decision stated in the notes is listed under a heading "Decisions".
C4: Every commitment in the notes, including informal ones stated in prose (for example "X said he would ..."), is one row of a table under a heading "Actions".
C5: The table columns are exactly Owner | Action | Due.
C6: Due is an ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD) or "none". A relative date such as "by Friday" is converted to an ISO date using the meeting date only when the notes make it clear; if there is no date or it is not clear, Due is "none".
C7: Decisions and actions use the words of the notes, and nothing appears that is not in the notes.
C8: Topics that were only discussed and not decided are not listed.
C9: The memo names the meeting date and the people present.
C10: The memo gives the next meeting date when the notes give one.
C11: The reviewer's report shows every criterion met (criteria met: n of m with n = m), and the writer applied every required change.
C12: The first line of the memo is one line starting "Summary:" that summarises the meeting, in the team's own words and using only facts from the notes (this is the one exception to "use the words of the notes"). After it come "Decisions", then "Actions". The meeting date, the people present and the next meeting date go after the Actions table.
C14: In the Actions table, the Owner column holds the owner's name as written in the notes (for example Priya), not an initial.
C15: In the Actions table, when the notes give an owner only as initials, the Owner cell reads those initials followed by "(full name not in the notes)", for example "JD (full name not in the notes)". Names given in full stay as written.
Rule (c): a playbook item may be the own words of a criterion added by an amendment the client confirmed (revise_playbook op="add": a step with that text, or a rule with it as then and no when; evidence: its id); confirmed now: C12, C14, C15. A criterion marked (not confirmed) counts once the client's yes is recorded (it reaches you as a criterion link); one marked (user-initiated) never does.
