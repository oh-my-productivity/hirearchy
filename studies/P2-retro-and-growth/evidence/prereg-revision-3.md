## PF-0: what iteration 3's self-check observed (a known product finding; its mechanism, not a new defect)

tools (a hand edit or the terminal)` and the classifier read `PF-0 0`, where iteration 2's run had reproduced PF-0. Six throwaway builds
then showed that the label depends on sub-second timing. The product gives `by lead, or outside the tools (the event log does not reach
back)` exactly when the log's earliest record (CLI only) falls in the same whole second as the older commit and after it in microseconds
(for example 08:33:58.380824 against 08:33:58). It gives the design's label when that record is a second earlier (08:33:55.896817 against
08:33:56). This matches 13.2 reading 1's harness bug of iteration 2 (microseconds against whole seconds), not iteration 1's reading
"MCP-path records only".

  product's label for each timing and the classifier for each output: `PF-0 1` on the same-second build, `PF-0 0 new 0` on the
  earlier-second one.
