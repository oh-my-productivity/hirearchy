Acceptance criteria now:

C2: At most 400 words as counted by read.
C3: The memo has a section titled "Decisions" that lists every decision in the notes, worded as in the notes.
C4: The memo has a section titled "Actions" with a markdown table with the columns Owner | Action | Due, with one row for every commitment in the notes, including informal ones written in prose ("X said he would ..."), worded as in the notes.
C5: Due is an ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD). A relative deadline such as "before Friday" becomes a date only when the notes and the meeting date make it clear; otherwise, and when the notes give no date, Due is "none".
C6: A name that is not in the attendee list is used as written in the notes and marked "(not in attendee list)".
C7: Nothing appears in the memo that is not in the notes file.
C8: The lead's check against the notes shows every decision and every commitment covered (n of m with n = m).
C9: The memo starts with a one-line "Summary:" of the meeting, followed by the "Decisions" section, then the "Actions" section, in that order.
C10: The Actions table leaves out any line where nobody has to do anything, such as a statement that something stays as it is.
