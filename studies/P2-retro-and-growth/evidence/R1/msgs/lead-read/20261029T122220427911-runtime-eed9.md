C2: The memo has a Decisions section listing every decision made in the named notes file, and nothing from other files.

C4: Every commitment in the notes is a row of the table, both those labelled ACTION and those stated in prose, such as someone saying they would do something.
C5: Each Due value is an ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD). A relative deadline such as 'by Friday' is turned into a date only when the notes make the date clear; if the notes give no date, Due is 'none'.
C6: Each Owner value uses the name exactly as written in the notes.
C7: Every statement in the memo is traceable to the notes file, and anything unclear (no owner, an unconfirmed decision) is listed under a final heading 'Unclear' instead of being guessed.
