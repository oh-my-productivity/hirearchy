## Goal
Research the five apps, write the analysis for a team building a competing self-hosted note app, have it fact-checked by someone other than the writer, apply required changes, and report the final file.

Deliverable: Competitor analysis with a comparison table (one row per app) and a final section titled "Where we can win" (markdown, at most 1500 words). Report its path as an artifact.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Covers exactly Obsidian, Logseq, Joplin, Trilium Notes and AnyType, and no other products.
2. At most 1500 words as counted by read.
3. A comparison table with one row per app and columns for license, self-hosting method, sync and collaboration, editor and format, mobile apps, and E2E encryption.
4. A short section per app on strengths, weaknesses and best fit.
5. Every factual claim has a primary-source URL.
6. A final section titled "Where we can win".
7. The fact-checker verified at least 8 claims and the writer applied every required change.
