## Goal
Deliver the service `meeting-memo`: One short memo of the decisions and the who-does-what from one weekly meeting's notes file

## Request
- notes_file: 2026-10-05.md

## Acceptance criteria
- The memo is based only on the requested notes file and covers that meeting only.
- At most 400 words as counted by read.
- Format is markdown with a section "Decisions" and a section "Action items".
- The Decisions section lists every decision stated in the notes file, each in one line, and no decision that is not in the notes.
- The Action items section is a markdown table with exactly the columns Owner | Action | Due, with one row for every commitment in the notes file, including informal ones stated in prose (for example 'I'll sort it out'); no row for something nobody committed to.
- Every Due value is an ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD) or the word none when the notes give no date; a date is never invented, and an owner or date that the notes leave unclear is listed in a final section titled 'Open questions'.
- The reviewer's report shows every criterion met (criteria met: n of m with n = m) and every decision and commitment in the notes file checked against the memo (n of m, n = m), and the writer applied every required change.
