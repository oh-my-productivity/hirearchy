Acceptance criteria now:
C1: The memo covers only the meeting in the requested notes file and nothing else.
C2: At most 400 words as counted by read.
C3: The memo is markdown with a section titled "Decisions" and a section titled "Action items".
C4: Every decision recorded in the notes, including small ones such as a schedule change, is listed under Decisions, with its reason when the notes give one.
C5: Action items is a table with exactly the columns Owner | Action | Due.
C6: Every commitment in the notes is a row of the table, including informal ones said in prose without an ACTION label (anyone who said they would do something).
C7: Each row names the person the notes name as the owner.
C8: Due is an ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD). A vague deadline (for example 'end of next week' or 'the day before the freeze') is turned into a date only when the notes make the date clear from the meeting date or other dates in the notes; otherwise Due is 'none'. Due is 'none' when the notes give no deadline.
C9: No invented decisions, owners, dates or reasons: nothing is stated that the notes do not say.
C10: The reviewer's report shows every criterion met (criteria met: n of m with n = m) after checking the memo against the whole notes file, and the writer applied every required change.
C11: The memo starts with a one-line 'Summary:' of the meeting, followed by the Decisions section, then the Action items section, in that order.
