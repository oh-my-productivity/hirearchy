Acceptance criteria now:
C1: Covers only the meeting in the named notes file, and nothing from other files.
C2: At most 400 words as counted by read.
C3: Has a section titled "Decisions" that lists every firm decision made in the meeting; items that were discussed but left open are not listed as decisions.
C4: Has a section titled "Action items" that is a markdown table with exactly the columns Owner | Action | Due.
C5: The table has one row for every commitment in the notes, including informal ones stated in prose (for example "X offered to ..." or "X said he would ...") and those labelled ACTION.
C6: Every Due value is an ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD) or the word none. A vague or relative deadline (for example "next week", "by Friday") is turned into a date when the notes make it clear, otherwise it is none.
C7: Every decision and every table row is traceable to the notes file, and nothing is invented.
C8: The reviewer's report shows every criterion met (criteria met: n of m with n = m), and the writer applied every required change.
C9: The memo starts with one line beginning 'Summary:' that summarizes the meeting, followed by the Decisions section and then the Action items section.
