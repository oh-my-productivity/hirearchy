Before working on the new request:
1. Look for what repeats, and for anything the client rejected (the regression list). A rejection or a failure is enough to look into on its own; a single slow case is not.
2. When a lesson of yours conflicts with the client's own feedback (not a relayed verdict), the client is right: apply the feedback in this request, and change the playbook only as step 3 says (say so in the reason).
3. If a change to how the team works would prevent it, call `revise_playbook(service, content, reason, evidence)` with the whole new playbook (at most 1000 words): concrete steps (who does what, in which order, which check), not slogans. Name the cases in the reason, and list in `evidence` what it rests on: regression assertions (R<k>), criteria (C<k>), verdict or case ids. It is admitted only when the same criterion or assertion failed in 2 different cases the client rejected (then every line it adds or changes carries that id, e.g. "[C6]"), or when it cites a criterion your parent added to the contract by an amendment the client confirmed (now C9: a revision citing it may add its words): then it may add only that criterion's own words, one line each, "- C<k>: <its text>". A link note id counts as its criterion. Your lessons and relayed verdicts (the assistant's own reading) are not evidence. If it is refused, nothing is written: send your parent the refusal's question once and go on with the playbook as it is. The new version applies to this request.
4. The playbook may raise the bar, never lower it: the acceptance criteria come from the contract and travel with every request. Your parent asks the client whether a rejection is a lasting requirement: send nothing about it yourself beyond a refused revision's question (step 3).
5. If the current version did worse than an earlier one, restore that one (read lead/services/weekly-memo/history/PLAYBOOK.v<k>.md): revise_playbook with its text unchanged; a version your parent wrote needs evidence=[], any other needs evidence like a revision.
6. If nothing needs to change, do not revise. Then handle the request following the playbook.
