Acceptance criteria now:
C1: Covers only the meeting in the requested notes file and nothing else.
C2: At most 400 words as counted by read.
C3: Has a section titled Decisions that lists every decision the notes record as made (decided or agreed), one line each, and no item that is not a decision.
C4: Has a section titled Actions with a markdown table whose columns are exactly Owner | Action | Due.
C5: The table has one row for every commitment in the notes, including informal ones stated in prose (for example someone saying they will look into something), not only items labelled ACTION.
C6: Due is an ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD) when the notes give a date, or a deadline that resolves to one from the meeting date (a named weekday means that weekday of the meeting's week; the next meeting means the date the notes give for it). Due is 'none' when the notes give no date, say no hard date, or give only a loose period such as 'this week'.
C7: Every decision and every table row is traceable to the notes file; no owner, action or date is invented.
C8: The reviewer's report shows every criterion met (criteria met: n of m with n = m), and the writer applied every required change.
C9: Starts with a one-line 'Summary:' of the meeting, then the Decisions section, then the Actions table, in that order.
