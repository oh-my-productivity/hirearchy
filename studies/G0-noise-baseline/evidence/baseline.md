Reading the tables. Rows are measures; columns are the streams (grouped by arm, then skill stratum), then the group cells over VALID streams only.
A numeric cell over 3 or more streams is `min / median / max` (the median of an even count is the mean of the two middle values); a rate is the pooled `k of n` (`n/a (0 of 0)` when the pooled denominator is 0); a yes/no value is `k of n` streams; a category is a count per value. A cell over fewer than 3 valid streams lists its values and says `spread n/a (k of 3)` (design 20.1). `missing (...)` means the stream has no such value (its file is absent or does not hold the key): it is never replaced by a number, and a group cell says how many streams were missing. No test of significance is made (design 20.5).
