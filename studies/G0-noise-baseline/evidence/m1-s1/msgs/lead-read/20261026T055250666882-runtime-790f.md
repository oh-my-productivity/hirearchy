Acceptance criteria now:
C1: Covers only the meeting in the requested notes file and nothing else.
C2: At most 400 words as counted by read.
C4: Decisions lists every decision recorded in the notes file, and no decision that is not in it.
C5: Action items is a markdown table with the columns Owner | Action | Due, with one row for every commitment in the notes, including informal ones made in prose (someone said they would do something), not only lines labelled ACTION.
C6: Every row has a named person as Owner.
C7: Due is an ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD). A vague deadline ('before Friday', 'next week') becomes an ISO date only when the notes make the date clear, and the original wording is kept in the Action cell; otherwise Due is 'none'. No date is ever invented.
C8: Every statement is traceable to the notes file.
C9: The reviewer's report shows every criterion met (criteria met: n of m with n = m), and the writer applied every required change.
C10: Start the memo with a one-line 'Summary:' of the meeting, then the Decisions, then the Action items. Besides a title line with the meeting date, the memo has exactly these three parts.
