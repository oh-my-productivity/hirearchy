Before working on the new request:
1. Look for what repeats, and for anything the client rejected. A rejection or a failure is enough on its own; a single slow case is not.
2. If a change to how the team works would prevent it, call `revise_playbook(service, content, reason)` with the whole new playbook (at most 1000 words): concrete steps (who does what, in which order, which check), not slogans. Name the cases in the reason. The new version applies to this request.
3. The playbook may raise the bar, never lower it: the acceptance criteria come from the contract and travel with every request. If the client's feedback is a lasting requirement, also tell your parent (`send`) so it can add it to the contract.
4. If the current version did worse than an earlier one, restore that one (read lead/services/weekly-memo/history/PLAYBOOK.v<k>.md).
5. If nothing needs to change, do not revise. Then handle the request following the playbook.
