# Platform team weekly, Monday 12 October 2026

Present: Priya, Sam, Lee, Marta, Dev.

Opening item was the rate limiter RFC. Priya had circulated it on Friday and most of the discussion was about whether to count requests per API key or per customer account. Token bucket per API key won out because it matches how customers already think about their keys and it keeps one noisy integration from starving the others. The team decided to approve the RFC with the token bucket design and to ship it behind a feature flag first.

We then talked about last Thursday's outage, when the billing service was unavailable for about forty minutes after a bad config push. The tone was constructive. We decided the postmortem will be blameless and will be shared with the whole company, not just engineering, because several customer-facing teams were asked about it. ACTION: Dev to write the postmortem for the Oct 8 outage by Oct 14.

Marta brought up the customer demo scheduled for the week of 19 October. Because the demo environment is the same cluster we deploy to, the team decided on a deploy freeze on Oct 22 and Oct 23 so nothing changes under the presenter. ACTION: Marta to send the demo agenda to the customer by Oct 16.

Priya said she is ready to start building. ACTION: Priya to start implementing the rate limiter behind a feature flag by Oct 20.

Sam gave a quick update on CI: the cache theory held up, but the fix touches the runner image, so it needs more than a quick patch. On staging, he mentioned that the test data is out of date and will probably sort it out himself so that it's ready the day before the freeze starts.

On the staffing side, Lee mentioned that he doesn't mind carrying the pager during the demo week, so he'll swap shifts with Dev, who would rather be free to present the demo. Nobody had a problem with that.

Meeting closed at 10:50. Next meeting Monday 19 October.
