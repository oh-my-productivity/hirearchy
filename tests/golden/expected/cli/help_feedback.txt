usage: hirearchy feedback [-h] [--note NOTE] [--source {human,relayed}] [--criterion ID]
                          [--confirms VERDICT_ID]
                          task_id {accept,reject}

positional arguments:
  task_id
  {accept,reject}

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --note NOTE           what was wrong (required for reject)
  --source {human,relayed}
                        human (default): your own judgement; relayed: someone else's reading of
                        the delivery
  --criterion ID        what a rejection was about: C<k> (a contract criterion), R<k> (an earlier
                        rejection it repeats) or none (no lasting rule) (an answer that changes no
                        criterion)
  --confirms VERDICT_ID
                        record --criterion for an earlier rejection (its message id) instead of a
                        new verdict; --note is your answer
